FAQ

Do I need an electrician?

No. Every shelf ships with a plug-in driver and a slim cord you can run down the wall in the included paintable cover. Want it hardwired? Each shelf is hardwire-ready for your electrician, no extra cost.

How much weight can it hold?

Our target spec is up to 50 lb per shelf when the bracket is screwed into two studs. Books, speakers, decor and small consoles are fine.

How do I measure?

Measure the wall space and subtract 2 in on each side if it sits between walls. Mark your studs. Our install guide walks you through it in 5 minutes.

Can I change the light later?

Yes. The remote dims and turns the light on and off. Pick RGB + White if you want color scenes, or add the smart controller for Alexa and Google Home.

How long does it take?

Each shelf is made to order. It ships in 10-14 business days and arrives ready to hang, with the LED already installed.

What if it arrives damaged?

Send us a photo within 7 days and we remake it at no cost. Every shelf is packed in a rigid box with corner protection.
