FAQ

How long does it take?

Each shelf is made to order. It ships in 10-14 business days and arrives ready to hang, with the LED already installed.

What if it arrives damaged?

Send us a photo within 7 days and we remake it at no cost. Every shelf is packed in a rigid box with corner protection.

Do the modules fit any TV?

The cubes are 24 x 24 x 16 in. A 3-cube console (72 in) sits well under TVs up to 75 in; a 4-cube console (96 in) under TVs up to 85 in.

Do you install?

In select areas our install partners can hang it for you. Everywhere else it is a 30-minute DIY job with a drill.
