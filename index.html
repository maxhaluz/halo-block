Questions

Do I need an electrician?

No. Each shelf ships with a plug-in adapter that goes into a standard wall outlet.

Where does the cord go?

A thin cord leaves the underside of the shelf and runs down to the outlet. A paintable cord cover makes it disappear on most walls.

What sizes can I order?

Any length from 24 to 96 inches, by the inch, in 8, 10 or 12 inch depth.

When does it ship?

Every shelf is made to order. We confirm your ship date by email before you pay anything.
